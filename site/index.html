The Gauntlet

A music league for you and your friends. Start a league, pick a song for each round's category, listen to the playlist together, rank everyone's picks, and see who wins.

Help and support

Questions, problems or feedback? Email thegauntletapp@gmail.com and we'll get back to you.

Reporting a player or comment

In the app, tap ••• next to a player on the league screen, or Report next to a comment. We review every report within 24 hours. You can also block a player from the same menu.

Deleting your account

Open the app, tap your avatar to open Settings, then tap Delete account. This removes your profile and everything you've added. If you can't get into the app, email us and we'll delete it for you.
