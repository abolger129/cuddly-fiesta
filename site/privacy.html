The Gauntlet

Privacy policy

Last updated October 28, 2026

The Gauntlet is a music league game you play with friends. This policy explains what information the app keeps, why, and what you can do about it. The short version: we keep only what the game needs, we don't show ads, we don't track you across other apps or websites, and we never sell your information.

What we keep

  • Your account. Your email address, and if you sign in with Apple or Google, the account ID they give us. If you use Sign in with Apple and choose to hide your email, we only see Apple's relay address.
  • Your profile. The display name you choose and the avatar you draw. Other players in your leagues can see these.
  • What you do in the game. The leagues you create or join, the songs you pick (title, artist, album art and a Spotify or YouTube link), your rankings, your guesses, your comments, your category ideas, and players you block. Other players in the same league see your songs, rankings and comments once a round is finished.
  • Reports. If you report a player or comment, we keep the report and a copy of what was reported so we can review it.
  • Notifications. If you allow notifications, we keep your device's push token so we can tell you when a round opens or is about to close.

What stays on your phone

If you connect Spotify to make league playlists, the Spotify sign-in is stored only on your device, in its secure storage. We use it to search for songs and to create playlists on your Spotify account when you ask. We don't store your Spotify listening history.

Who helps us run the app

  • Supabase hosts our database and handles sign-in.
  • Expo delivers push notifications and app updates.
  • Spotify provides song search and playlists, if you connect it.
  • Apple and Google handle sign-in if you choose them.
  • YouTube links open on YouTube if you tap them.

They only get what they need to do their part. We don't share your information with anyone else, unless the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while you have an account. When you delete your account, your profile, avatar, songs, rankings, guesses, comments and push tokens are deleted right away. Leagues you host pass to another player, or are deleted if nobody else is in them. Reports you made are kept without your name so we can still act on them.

Your choices

  • Change your name or avatar any time in Settings.
  • Turn off notifications in your phone's settings.
  • Block a player so you don't see their comments, name or avatar.
  • Delete your account in the app: Settings → Delete account.
  • Email us to ask for a copy of your information, to correct it, or to delete it.

Children

The Gauntlet is not meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you think a child under 13 has an account, email us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If the change is important, we'll tell you in the app.
