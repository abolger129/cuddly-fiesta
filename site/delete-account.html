The Gauntlet

Delete your account

To request that your The Gauntlet account and the data linked to it be deleted, follow these steps.

How to request deletion by email

  1. Open the email app you use with the address you sign in to The Gauntlet with.
  2. Write a new email to thegauntletapp@gmail.com. That link opens a draft with everything filled in.
  3. Use the subject Delete my account and data.
  4. In the message, say: Please delete my The Gauntlet account and all data linked to it.
  5. Send it from the same email address you sign in with. That is how we confirm the account is yours. If you sign in with Apple and hide your email address, or use Google, also write the name shown on your profile and which sign-in you use.
  6. We'll reply to confirm once your account and data have been deleted. If we can't match the email to an account, we'll write back to ask for more detail.

If you only want some data removed, for example to correct or delete part of what we hold without closing your account, say which in the same email.

Prefer to do it yourself?

If you can still open the app, tap your avatar to open Settings, then tap Delete account. This deletes your account right away, with no email needed.

What gets deleted

Your profile, avatar, songs, rankings, guesses, comments and push notification tokens. Leagues you host pass to another player, or are deleted if nobody else is in them.

What we keep

If you reported a player or comment, we keep the report without your name, so we can still act on it.
